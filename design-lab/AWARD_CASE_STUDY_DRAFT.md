# SUM — Make Reality Summonable
## Draft design case study, not a claimed award or official submission

### The problem

Digital activity systems tend to separate a promise, an agreement, its evidence, and its result into disconnected screens. Social challenges can be emotionally engaging but ambiguous; agreement and verification interfaces are often precise yet impersonal. SUM explores a more comprehensible way of connecting these steps.

### The original idea

A person becomes a Summoner by turning a real-life intention into a Quest. Pico, the AI Familiar, is not an automated winner but a companion who helps the user articulate the objective, identify missing conditions and understand evidence. SUM's recurring visual instrument is a spatial Gate that responds to actual product state. One continuing Quest Artifact changes its presentation as the same Quest moves through formation, shared terms, proof and Chronicle.

### The form

The brand combines a living opalescent threshold, soft jade and porcelain materials, a recognizable Familiar silhouette and a restrained typographic contrast. Different environments have different layouts and tasks, while their symbols, materials, interaction patterns and object identity remain consistent. GPU rendering is a progressive enhancement; accessible navigation, clear states and standard form controls work without it.

### Intended impact

The interaction makes a normally abstract workflow understandable as one continuous journey, with uncertainty and consent explicitly represented. The visual design aims to make real-world participation inviting without pretending that an AI explanation replaces evidence or agreement.

### Why the work is distinctive

The Gate is not only an ornament: its expression is tied to the live editor's blank, input, processing, clarification, ready-for-review and error states. The reusable Artifact carries a consistent identity across chapters. Pico has purposeful expressions rather than unrelated decorative reactions.

### Claims that require later independent evidence

- Whether first-time users understand the concept in five seconds.
- Whether an international audience recognizes SUM without its logo.
- Whether Quest completion and informed consent improve.
- Whether user delight and retention improve.
- Real-user Core Web Vitals and device-level rendering coverage.

These statements are goals, not measured outcomes. Do not state or imply an award was received, the project was entered, or a jury endorsed the work.

### Short competition description — editorial draft

SUM turns everyday intentions into real-world Quests that people can understand and share. A spatial Summoning Gate creates a distinctive entry to the experience, while Pico, an AI Familiar, helps users clarify objectives, participants and evidence without taking away their decision-making authority. A persistent Quest Artifact carries the identity of each challenge from initial idea through shared agreement, proof and Chronicle. SUM's visual language combines opalescent spatial forms, warm tactile materials, precise typography and purposeful character expressions. Animations reflect real interface states, and important tasks remain usable with reduced motion or without WebGL. The concept connects imagination and accountability: real actions become memorable shared stories, while uncertainty, consent and evidence remain visible. Any reported user impact would require independent measurement.
