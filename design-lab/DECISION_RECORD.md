# SUM / The Living Threshold

**Status:** Internal creative exploration, not an award nomination or external validation.

**Reference:** Red Dot Award, Brands & Communication Design evaluates Idea (originality), Form (aesthetic and functional execution), and Impact (clarity, differentiation and emotion). https://www.red-dot.org/bcd/red-dot-jury

## Why this direction

SUM is not a badminton microsite, paper-certificate generator, casino, or AI dashboard. Users are Summoners; real-world intentions become Quests; Pico is the Familiar; shared consent and evidence lead to a Chronicle. Previous map and editorial-club iterations each weakened one dimension of the brand.

The differentiator is **a living threshold between intention and fair real-world action**.

## The shared visual object

- SUM Sigil: open hexagon and central point of intention.
- Gate: opalescent architectural aperture above a small dimensional stage.
- Artifact: the same jade and porcelain materials, sigil and stage markings.
- Pico: one brand silhouette with event-specific expression.
- Three materials: warm porcelain (human), brushed jade (action), opal (guidance).
- Two typographic roles: confident readable sans plus limited Fraunces for transformation.
- No fake geolocation, fake verified score, fabricated model confidence or decorative crypto claims.

## Stage ownership

| Stage | Real source | Safe visual meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Dormant | empty editor | waiting for an idea |
| Listening | nonempty text | unfinished intention, NOT confirmed AI understanding |
| Working | actual request pending | drafting, NOT publishing |
| Clarifying | real returned incomplete draft | unresolved conditions |
| Formed | real readyToPublish | draft available for user review, NOT published |
| Error | real error | needs attention, no success |
| Pact/Proof/Chronicle | authenticated live record OR explicitly marked sample | provenance and uncertainty remain visible |

Business state, consent, credit movement, evidence storage, authentication and judgment backend must remain unchanged.

## Performance and safety commitments

- Real Three.js geometry, not a staged video.
- Lazy GPU initialization near the viewport; max pixel ratio 1.5, approximately 30 FPS.
- Stop frame work offscreen; dispose renderer and resources on unmount.
- Readable CSS fallback if WebGL is unavailable, and reduced-motion support.
- No business API mutations from illustrative concept controls.
- Mobile input must remain easy to reach.

## Required sign-off

- Actual screenshots across six pages, desktops and phones.
- Cross-width overflow, keyboard and click testing.
- State-driven portal and continuity of the Quest Artifact.
- Clear demo/privacy distinctions.
- Typecheck, lint, CSS parse, production build and browser validation from one exact Git commit.
- Independent critic must provide a defect report. Internal scores cannot substitute for professional evaluation.
- No fabricated awards, usability findings or field Core Web Vitals.
