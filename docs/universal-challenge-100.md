# Universal Challenge 100 Catalog

Purpose: prove "anything can become a challenge" by routing very different prompts into executable protocol classes. These are designed to compile into ProtocolSpecV2 and be judgeable by one of the product paths: video event, GPS, public oracle, receipt/photo, screenshot/platform metric, text answer, or manual review fallback. This catalog is not a claim that all 100 have production E2E settlement proof yet.
